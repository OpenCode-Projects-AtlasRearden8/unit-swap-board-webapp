Unit Swap Board

Organize shift swaps within your own squad — a notebook that lives in this browser.

Education-only practice tool. This is not a scheduling-of-record system and it is not medical advice. Nothing here changes an official schedule: every swap must follow your facility's own process and get official sign-off. All data is stored only in this browser (localStorage). No accounts, no cloud.

Your squad

Add the nurses you're trading with. Removing someone also removes their open cards and unassigns their slots.

Shift grid

Write down who is pencilled into each slot and mark slots that need coverage. Edit the day labels to match your schedule.

Requests & offers

A request asks for coverage of a slot. An offer says a nurse can cover that slot (optionally naming a trade-back slot for a straight trade). Give your card a thumbs-up when you've actually talked to your squad.

Possible matches

A match pairs a request with an offer on the same slot for different nurses. When both cards have a thumbs-up, you can record the trade as covered — then everyone writes it down themselves and prints a confirmation note.

Covered trades (your own written record)

Fairness counter

Counts covered trades from this board: given = you covered for someone, received = someone covered for you.
